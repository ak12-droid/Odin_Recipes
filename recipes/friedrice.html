Uncle Roger Fried Rice

Description

A fried rice recipe from the youtuber Uncle Roger. Born from his frustrations with others fried rice recipes

Ingredients

  • Cooking oil
  • 5 cloves of garlic chopped
  • 1 full shallot chopped
  • 1 full egg + one egg yolk
  • Green onions diced
  • Chopped chile (optional)
  • Cooked rice (preferably old rice)
  • Soy sauce
  • Asian sesame oil
  • MSG

Instructions

  1. Coat wok with oil until it's smoking
  2. Add a little more oil and spread around
  3. Add garlic and shallots. Fry until fragrant
  4. Add egg and stir around until almost solid
  5. Add rice to desired amount (add more oil if dry)
  6. Pour soy sauce until ancestors are proud
  7. Add the sesame oil (flatten rice to ensure it doesn't clump)
  8. Sprinkle in generous amount of MSG
  9. Toss in green onion and chile
  10. Serve when rice is done
